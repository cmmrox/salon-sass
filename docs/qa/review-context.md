# Review context

Who uses this product, what matters to them, and what it is compared with. QA uses this when
writing a product review; the method is in the `salon-qa` skill, `references/product-review.md`.

## Who you are when you review

| Person | Situation |
| --- | --- |
| A customer | On a small Android phone, on mobile data, booking in a spare minute, in the later React Native app after salon web release. No customer website is delivered |
| A receptionist | At the front desk on a busy Saturday, phone ringing, a walk-in waiting, using a tablet |
| A professional | Between clients, checking the next appointment one-handed |
| A salon owner | At the end of the day, wanting to know what was earned and what needs attention |
| The operator | Setting up a new salon, or working out what went wrong at one |

## What matters here in particular

- **Fit for the place:** Sri Lankan phone numbers, rupees, local holidays including Poya days,
  WhatsApp as the normal way to reach a salon, patchy mobile data, small Android phones.
- **The salon's interest:** fewer no-shows, fewer phone calls and less double handling. That is why
  the salon is paying.
- **Trust at the point of payment:** the deposit, the cancellation policy and the exact amount are
  plain before the customer pays, and the confirmation is unmistakable afterwards.
- **A salon's own identity:** the customer should feel they are dealing with their salon, not with a
  platform. No other salon is ever visible.

## What it is compared with

| Product | Why it is the comparison |
| --- | --- |
| Fresha | The international leader, priced for Sri Lanka, and the one a salon here is most likely to have tried |
| Booksy, Vagaro | Established international products with mature booking and calendar flows |
| Local products named in BRD section 5 | What a Sri Lankan salon is choosing between today |

The BRD's capability comparison (section 6) is the starting point. Their public booking pages and
help articles show current behaviour. Use the public pages only; do not create accounts with real
personal data.
