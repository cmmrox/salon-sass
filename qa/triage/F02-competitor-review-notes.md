Primary public documentation refreshed on 2026-10-14. No competitor account was created and no real personal data was used. These are comparison inputs, not a completed manual feature review or approved scope changes.

| Product/document | Relevant current documented behavior | Compare on the final F02 screens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Fresha permission roles](https://www.fresha.com/help-center/knowledge-base/team/100687-create-permission-roles) | Permissions are presented by calendar, sales, client, report and other business areas, with assigned staff and a brief access overview | Check whether an Owner understands the fixed role and Professional grant before changing access; custom role editing remains outside F02 |
| [Booksy staff permissions](https://support.booksy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/16535688940946-How-do-I-manage-staff-permissions) | Five staff levels have explicit capability descriptions; some role-specific calendar/client access can be customized | Compare role wording, scope explanation and safe read-only Manager presentation for a busy front desk; Booksy's Manager management powers do not change this product's Owner-only rule |
| [Vagaro verification](https://support.vagaro.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005413687-Two-Factor-or-Two-Step-Authentication) | Describes six-digit verification, remembered-device cadence, verified completion and new-device notification | Compare clarity of code lifetime, resend, current devices and successful completion. F02 Owner authenticator verification is its own policy; device alerts/recovery are proposals only |
| [Vagaro customer account closure](https://support.vagaro.com/hc/en-us/articles/1260806204150-Close-Your-Vagaro-Account-for-Customers-of-a-Vagaro-Business) | Customer profile includes account-management deletion and a confirmation; documents future appointments/membership/invoice prerequisites and a further information-deletion request | Compare discoverability and clear salon-specific deletion consequences with F02's synchronous data erasure/export. Booking/payment prerequisites belong to later features and are not invented here |
